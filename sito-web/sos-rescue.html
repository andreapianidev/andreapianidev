SOS GPS Rescue

Personal safety on your iPhone: hold one button and your position, your emergency details and a call for help reach the people you trust — even when you are off the grid.

View on the App Store

SOS GPS Rescue is a support tool. It does not replace the emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US).

What it does

SOS with countdown

Hold the button for three seconds. A countdown starts so you can cancel a false alarm before anything is sent.

Fall & crash detection

Accelerometer and barometer recognise hard falls and high-energy vehicle impacts, then start the emergency flow on their own.

Acoustic beacon

Pulsed sound and SOS flash to be located, an anti-assault siren, or a Morse strobe using only the LED and the screen.

Offline maps

Save a static image of your area and your breadcrumb trail, so you keep your bearings with no signal.

Duress PIN

A second PIN that shows an innocuous screen while it quietly prepares the SOS in the background.

Dead Man's Switch

Periodic check-ins on your safety. Miss the ping and the countdown begins automatically.

Mesh Radar

Finds nearby devices running the app over the local network, with no cellular coverage required.

Live Activity

The countdown and emergency state stay visible on the Dynamic Island and the Lock Screen.

Privacy

No account, no sign-up, no analytics server. Your position, your contacts and your medical details stay on your iPhone and are only used to compose the message you send. Read the full privacy policy.
